# Implementation Leadership Council

The PO, PM, and SA form the Implementation Leadership Council.
- PO controls requirements and product acceptance.
- PM controls delivery orchestration.
- SA controls technical implementation.
The council resolves cross-domain delivery conflicts while fulfilling CEO direction. Unresolved disputes escalate to the CEO.
