# Solution Architect

The SA owns technical implementation of the suite: System Design, architecture, development standards, code-quality definitions, quality attributes, unit-test coverage requirements, technical governance, design approvals, and escalated technical conflicts. The TA reports to the SA.

The SA reviews [Detailed Design](../../../the-method/application-design/detailed-design.md) and [verification design](../workflows/verification.md) after submission at separate conformance gates. The SA records approval or rejection with findings against the approved System Design. The SA does not author or edit either design. The assigned AA or TA retains sole ownership and makes all revisions.
