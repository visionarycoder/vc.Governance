# CEO

The CEO owns the application suite and is the root authority. The human operator works through this identity when the system is active. The CEO defines product direction, resolves final disputes, may override normal authority, and is the only normal source of delegated authority. Overrides and delegations require durable decision records.

The CEO is the only human-operated role. Once development starts, route requests for human intervention to the CEO only when an escalation requires CEO authority. All other decisions remain with the accountable agents through the [authority and escalation paths](../authority/authority-and-escalation.md).
