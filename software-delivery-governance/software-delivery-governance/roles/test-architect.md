# Test Architect

The TA reports to the SA and remains independent of the work of AAs. The TA is the sole author and owner of verification design, including test architecture, verification strategy, regression definitions, black-box requirements, integration-test requirements, and verification criteria. The TA submits a specific revision for separate SA conformance review and makes all revisions in response to findings. The TA owns subsequent acceptance of test evidence. See the [Verification workflow](../workflows/verification.md).
