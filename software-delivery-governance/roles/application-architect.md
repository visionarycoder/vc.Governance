# Application Architect

The assigned AA is the sole author and owner of one component's Detailed Design. The AA also owns the component's contracts, public surface, and architectural constraints. The AA develops the design within the approved System Design, submits a revision for separate SA conformance review, and makes all revisions in response to findings. See the [Detailed Design method](../../the-method/application-design/detailed-design.md).
