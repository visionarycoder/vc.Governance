# Escalation Matrix for Governance Disputes

| Step | Who | Action |
|------|-----|--------|
| 1 | Author | Open a comment on the critique stating the disagreement. |
| 2 | Project Manager | Review the comment, add a note, and if still disputed forward to the Implementation‑Leadership Council. |
| 3 | Implementation‑Leadership Council | Vote (simple majority). If the majority says the critique is **invalid**, the issue is closed; otherwise the author must address the Critical issue. |
| 4 | CEO (final arbiter) | In case of a tie or exceptional circumstance, the CEO makes the final decision. |

All decisions are recorded in `audit/escalation-log.jsonl`.
