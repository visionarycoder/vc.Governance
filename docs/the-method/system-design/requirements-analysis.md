# Requirements Analysis

## Goal
Understand the required behavior and expose architectural pressure without converting requirements directly into functional components.

## SA instructions
1. Load the approved requirements relevant to the change.
2. Resolve terminology through the local ontology.
3. Identify required externally observable behavior.
4. Identify constraints, quality attributes, integrations, and dependencies explicitly stated by authoritative artifacts.
5. Identify ambiguity or conflict. Escalate requirement questions to the Product Owner rather than reinterpret them.
6. Record which existing components, contracts, and Surface entries may be affected.
7. Continue to volatility-analysis.

## Prohibited
- Do not edit requirements.
- Do not decompose the system by use case, feature, screen, workflow, or requirement grouping merely because those groupings are convenient.
- Do not invent missing requirements.
